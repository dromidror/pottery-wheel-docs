# Settings

*What you'll learn: the global settings that affect design and export.*

- **Wall thickness** (mm) — thickness of hollow walls; affects galleries and export.
- **Units** — display/entry in **mm** or **inch** (internal storage is always mm).
- **Theme** — Light, Dark, or Follow System.

=== "Android"
    Settings are in the app's settings screen.

=== "macOS"
    Settings open from the toolbar gear or **Settings…** (⌘,).
