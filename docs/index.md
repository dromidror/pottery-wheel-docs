# Pottery Wheel

Design tableware on a virtual wheel, build it up from parts — handles, lids, spouts,
bases — decorate it with text and relief **stamps**, and export **STL models** and
**casting molds** for 3D printing and slip casting.

This guide covers **both apps** from one place:

<span class="badge android">Android</span> the phone/tablet app &nbsp;&nbsp;
<span class="badge macos">macOS</span> the desktop app

Where the two differ (a tap vs a click, the bottom nav vs the menu bar), you'll see a
short note or tabbed steps.

## What you can make

- Cups, mugs, bowls, dishes, pots, tumblers, teapots — from built-in templates or from scratch.
- Multi-part ware: bodies with handles, lids with knobs, spouts, feet/bases.
- Interlocking **galleries** so lids seat correctly.
- **Stamps**: monograms/text and imported **relief** sprigs, wrapped to the curved surface.
- **Prototype STL** for printing the ware itself.
- **Casting molds**: single-stage molds and 2-stage (negative) masters for slip casting.
